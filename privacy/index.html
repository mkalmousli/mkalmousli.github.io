Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This policy covers mkalmousli.dev, al-mo.de, and the web apps, APIs, and other services hosted on their subdomains (collectively, the "Services"), operated by Mohamad Almousli ("I", "me"). By using the Services you agree to this policy.

Summary

I collect analytics and technical usage data to understand how the Services are used, to operate and improve them, and to keep them secure. I do not sell your data. Individual apps hosted here may collect additional data — where they do, they carry their own notice.

Data collected automatically

  • Request & server data: IP address, approximate location derived from it, date and time, requested URL, referrer, HTTP method and status, response size, user-agent, and TLS/connection metadata.
  • Device & browser: browser and OS family and version, language, screen and viewport size, device type.
  • Usage & analytics: pages viewed, navigation paths, session duration, events and interactions within apps, performance timings, and error diagnostics.

Cookies & local storage

The static landing page uses only strictly-necessary storage. Hosted apps and APIs may set cookies or use localStorage/IndexedDB for sessions, authentication, preferences, and analytics. You can block or clear these in your browser, though some features may then stop working.

Processors & third parties

  • Cloudflare — hosting (Cloudflare Pages / Workers), CDN, DNS, DDoS protection, and Web Analytics. Cloudflare processes request data on my behalf; see also their GDPR & data-processing terms.
  • Google Fonts — serves the JetBrains Mono webfont; loading it exposes your IP address to Google.
  • shields.io — serves project status badges; loading them exposes your IP address to that provider.
  • Self-hosted analytics and logging on al-mo.de infrastructure.

Why I process this data

  • Operating, delivering, and securing the Services (legitimate interest / performance of a service).
  • Measuring traffic and improving content and features (legitimate interest / consent where required).
  • Detecting abuse, debugging, and preventing fraud (legitimate interest).
  • Complying with legal obligations.

Retention

Raw logs are kept for a limited period (typically up to 90 days) and then deleted or aggregated. Aggregated analytics that cannot identify you may be kept longer.

Sharing

I do not sell personal data. It is shared only with the processors above, or where required by law, or to protect the Services and their users.

International transfers

Processors such as Cloudflare and Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, or to object to or restrict processing. To exercise these rights, or to opt out of analytics, contact mohamad@al-mo.de. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 16, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

External links

Links to GitHub, GitLab, Instagram, F-Droid, pub.dev and other sites are provided for convenience; those services have their own privacy practices.
