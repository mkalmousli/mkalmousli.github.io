What I built

Sole developer and maintainer. The app draws a persistent overlay with WindowManager (TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY) that survives outside the app, backed by a foreground Service so Android's background limits don't kill it. It hooks the system AudioManager and a ContentObserver to stay in sync with hardware volume changes, and persists per-orientation button positions and theme settings.

Recent work: migrated to a foreground service to fix background kills, rebuilt the UI on Material 3 with Material You dynamic colour and runtime Light/Dark/System theming, added the live-preview Themes screen, bubble-style drag-to-hide, and fixed a launch crash on Android 14+. Releases are reproducible builds verified by F-Droid.
